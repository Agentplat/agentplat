# Experiment title

Status: planned / executed / failed / superseded.
Evidence class: specify the kind of evidence.

## Question

State the hypothesis, decision it informs, and success/failure criteria.

## Method

Describe the baseline, treatments, workload, sample count, selection process,
independent evaluator and known sources of bias. Identify simulated components.

## Reproduce

Link the runner, configuration and tests. Give commands, dependencies, expected
cost and service prerequisites. Record source revision (including dirty state),
runtime/environment and seeds for an executed run. State missing information.

## Results and artifacts

Link the output and raw evidence locations. State what is committed versus
externally stored. Include failed attempts and exclusions. Separate actual
measurements, proxies and unmeasured values. Do not fill results for planned work.

## Interpretation and limits

Explain what the observations support, what remains untested, and the next
decision. Link any applicable preregistration or historical evidence boundary.
