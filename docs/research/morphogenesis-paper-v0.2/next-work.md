# Remaining work toward the strongest empirical publication

The v0.2 deliverable is a revised systems preprint with bounded local evidence.
The broader publication objective remains active. The following are substantive
research obligations, not reasons to obscure the results already obtained.

1. **Full competing-transition experiment.** Use two independently approved
   proposals, real Team/Work integration and durable stores. Observe whether
   owner admission admits both effects before head CAS, whether pre-commit work
   can execute, and how the losing transition is cleaned up. Keep the current
   smaller counterexample as a boundary probe, not a substitute.
2. **Real mandate expiry and drain.** Exercise expiry/revocation during the
   decision-to-effect interval and pending/in-flight work during retirement.
   The fixed denial and phase-order probes do not establish these properties.
3. **Four-condition mission harness.** Compare adequately capable fixed teams,
   minimally coordinated adaptation, a competent durable workflow/saga baseline,
   and governed Morphogenesis. Use the same proposal policy and owner controls;
   bill reserved specialists and governance to the same budget. Start with a
   deterministic task oracle, then add separately registered model-backed work.
4. **Pilot and freeze the confirmatory design.** Estimate variance and failure
   exposure, choose sample size, preserve negative outcomes, freeze fault schedules
   and analysis before confirmatory execution. No inference from repeated
   deterministic execution counts alone.
5. **Causal reconstruction and Room continuity.** Verify the entire typed chain,
   authentic source receipts, retained artifact versions and successor task
   dependencies. The current artifact check establishes addressability only.
6. **Publication package.** Select the target's format, finish citation metadata,
   archive code/data in an immutable release and DOI deposit, and publish the
   paper/demo links together. No deposit or public submission has been made.

No new paid inference or external staging is required for items 1-2. Preserve
the historical staging configuration's disabled status; any separate campaign
must identify its own design, environment and claim boundary.
