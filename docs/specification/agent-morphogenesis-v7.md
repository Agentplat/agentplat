# AgentPlat Agent Morphogenesis Governed Organizational Evolution V7

**Defines:** opt-in, governed evolution of a collective's complete topology from
persistent V3–V6 evidence. **Status:** source capability; no production or
organizational-improvement claim.

V7 reuses the closed Morphogenesis operator vocabulary, validated V1/V2
operations, Dynamic Topology successor epochs, Strategy Synthesis V5, Agent
Genesis V6, Membership, Team/Work and Action owners. It creates no alternate
authority plane.

## Persistent pattern and candidates

A pattern binds current topology/epoch and separate strategy, collective,
synthesis and genesis evidence across a policy-minimum number of consecutive
cycles. Candidates are content-free target topologies plus an exact operator
set, changed identities, provider/model diversity, authority concentration,
cost, risk, continuity and rollback digests. Population, team count, churn,
diversity and concentration limits fail closed.

## Comparison and compilation

Reproducible simulation binds candidates, current topology, simulator
implementation, environment, seed and interaction budget. Every estimate
contains capacity, cost, diversity, continuity, risk, rollback and authority
distribution. Ranking is deterministic and advisory.

Compilation accepts only validated Morphogenesis V2 plans, Dynamic Topology V1
requests or governed V1 operations. It creates an acyclic multi-owner DAG,
requires exact operator coverage and binds `sourceEpoch + 1`, continuity and
rollback. Steps declare their authority owner and compensation requirement.

## Governance, canary and execution

Agent, person or quorum review can authorize only the exact plan/state. Approval
starts a bounded organizational canary. Capability, continuity, diversity and
authority observations are idempotent. Policy controls stable/unsafe thresholds
and explicit stabilization, isolation or rollback.

The durable runtime persists intent before each owner effect, reuses stable
operation IDs, validates receipts and reconciles crashes. Failure compensates
applied reversible steps in reverse order. Owners remain Morphogenesis, Dynamic
Topology, Membership, Work and Action Authority.

## Integrations and persistence

Owner handoffs and Rooms/Mesh projections contain only IDs/digests and grant no
Membership, Work or Action Authority. PostgreSQL persists governance and
execution heads with semantic validation, revision/digest CAS and rollback
witness. Organizational lineage binds candidate, plan, authorization, epochs,
effects, compensation and final status without granting authority.

Passing deterministic tests establishes source behavior only.
