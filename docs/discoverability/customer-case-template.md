## Publication outline

Describe the original problem, implementation and observed behavior. Attribute
measurements to the team that collected them and distinguish measured results
from estimates or opinions. Include failed cases and the observation window.
State which results are reproducible and which depend on private data. Publish
only approved facts, quotations and identities. Do not imply that a local test
or a customer-specific deployment certifies general production readiness.
